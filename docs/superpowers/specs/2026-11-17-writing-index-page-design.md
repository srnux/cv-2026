# Writing index page: design

## Intent

The home page is too long and reads less like a CV, because the Writing section shows every article as a large card with a cover. The articles still matter as evidence of knowledge, so they stay visible on the home page, in a much more compact form, and the full presentation moves to its own page.

## Goals

- Home page Writing section becomes a compact text list of **all** articles, all immediately visible (no collapsing, no "show more"): date, title, one-line summary. No covers, no tags, no buttons per article. Each title links straight to its article.
- No "All articles" link under the list.
- New index page at `/writing/` and `/de/writing/` that shows today's full cards (cover, date, reading time, title, summary, tags, Read and Repository buttons).
- Header and footer "Articles" links, and the back link on every article page, point to the index page instead of `/#writing`.

## Non-goals

- No router, no React route. No change to the article pages beyond their back link.
- No pagination, filtering or tag pages.

## Anti-goals

- The articles must not vanish from the home page's prerendered HTML. CV screeners and LLMs read the home page first.
- The index page must not need JavaScript to show its content.

## Approaches

- **A. Compact list on home, full index at `/writing/` (chosen).** It keeps the knowledge signal on the CV page and gives the articles room elsewhere. Changed from the first sketch: the list on home shows all articles, not the latest three.
- B. Articles only on `/writing/`. Rejected: no sign of the writing on the home page.
- C. One line in About linking to `/writing/`. Rejected: easy to miss.

## Design

- **Index page is generated by `scripts/build-articles.ts`**, as static HTML in `public/writing/index.html` and `public/de/writing/index.html`, using the same chrome, fonts, inline language script and switcher as the article pages. It is generated like everything else under `public/writing/`, so it is never hand-edited. The German index lists every article; untranslated ones carry the same "English only" note the home card shows today.
- **Head:** its own title and description in both languages, canonical, hreflang pair, Open Graph. Added to the sitemap as a translated pair.
- **`Writing.tsx`** renders the compact list from the same `articles.json` and keeps `id="writing"` so existing anchors still work. Untranslated articles keep the "English only" marker on the German page. No "All articles" link.
- **Links:** `Header.tsx` and `Footer.tsx` point to `/writing/` (`/de/writing/` in German); the `writing` link in the generator's page chrome points to the index.
- **`public/llms.txt`** gets the index URL.
- **Tests:** `tests/build-output.test.ts` checks that both index pages exist in `dist/` with every article linked, and that the home page still contains every article title.
